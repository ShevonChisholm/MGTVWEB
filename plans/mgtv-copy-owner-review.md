## Integrity checks and retained boundaries

- `scripts/fixtures/copy-baseline.json` was captured before application edits. `pnpm check:copy` compares policy bodies, all product monetary inputs, plan lists/comparison, canonical IDs/routes/relationships, event/broadcast states, filter values, and hashes of existing state/access/playback/purchase modules.
- Computed news read-time may change with the revised article length; fixture publication/event dates were not rolled forward.
- Fixture metric fields remain internal only where existing sort/state behavior depends on them.
- No backend, integrations, real authentication/email/submissions, new recommendation algorithm, mobile app, payment changes, deployment, publication or commits were added.
- Unused legacy components were not edited merely to remove search matches.

See `plans/mgtv-copy-verification.md` for checks actually run and visual evidence, rather than assuming the accepted plan’s proposed checks have all passed.
